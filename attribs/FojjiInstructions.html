General Info

⚠️ An active Fojji Patreon Sub is also required to use the various Fojji Integrations.

Whilst we are authorized to include the Fojji Assigns Weakauras and their passwords with the sheet, we are not authorized to include the Raiding Packs (which are essential to functionality).

Without the Fojji Sub, you can still use the CD Planner to generate visual cooldown displays on each boss page as a way of coordinating your raid CDs, but we'd recommend using the Fojji WAs since they make life so much easier!
